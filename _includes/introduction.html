About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Computer Vision and Geometry Group (CVG) at ETH Zurich, Switzerland, working with Prof. Marc Pollefeys. Previously, I worked with Prof. Chen Change Loy as a Research Fellow at MMLab@NTU and S-Lab, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. I received my Ph.D. from Beijing Jiaotong University in 2023, advised by Profs. Chunyu Lin and Yao Zhao. I was also a visiting scholar in the Computer Graphics Department at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, advised by Vahid Babaei and Prof. Hans-Peter Seidel.

Research Interests

My recent research interests include unified multimodal models, spatial AI, and world models.
